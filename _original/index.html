FAQ

Questions before you connect.

How is Octavise different from Meta or Google Ads reporting?

Meta and Google show ad-platform activity. Octavise adds practice context: bookings, new patients, revenue, cancellations, referrals, and operating trends.

Which systems does Octavise connect to?

Octavise currently supports Nookal and Cliniko for practice management, plus Meta Ads and Google Ads for advertising data.

Is this exact patient-level attribution?

No. Octavise is designed to show practical performance context and correlation, not overclaim deterministic patient-level attribution.

Does Octavise modify campaigns or practice records?

No. The dashboard uses read-only access and does not change appointments, patient records, or ad campaigns.
